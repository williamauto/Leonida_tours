How to read this map

The map covers Miami Beach and Downtown, the causeway, Little Havana and Wynwood, the Everglades and the Florida Keys. Each pin links to a page with the full address, the coordinates, how confident we are about the match and which tour stops there.

Some locations are close together because the real city is compact: Ocean Drive to Brickell is about four miles. The Everglades and the Keys are the two outliers, and both are worth a full day.
