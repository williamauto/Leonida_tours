Itinerary

  1. +0:00

    1. Ocean Drive

    Inspired · the neon hotel strip

    Start on Ocean Drive, where pastel Art Deco hotels, hand-painted neon and the Clevelander corner light up the night. Stand where the first trailer opened, then watch the strip come alive.

  2. +0:25

    2. Lummus Park Beach

    Inspired · the beach scenes

    Candy-striped lifeguard towers, white sand and Atlantic surf. The crew photo stop: everyone lines up by the tower, and everyone sends it to someone.

  3. +0:40

    3. Venetian Causeway

    Inspired · the causeway drive

    Cross the bay on the old Venetian Causeway, the skyline climbing ahead of you. Eyes out of the window for this one.

  4. +0:55

    4. Downtown & Biscayne Blvd

    Inspired · the arena and the club

    The waterfront arena, a crown of changing light on the tallest tower, and the 24-hour superclub straight out of the second trailer.

  5. +1:10

    5. Wynwood

    Inspired · the arts district

    A 25-minute walk through Wynwood’s murals, galleries and warehouse streets. The walls change every month, so no two crews ever get the same backdrop.

  6. +1:40

    6. Little Haiti

    Inspired · the Caribbean quarter

    Bright murals, loud color and patties from the counter at the cultural complex. The stop nobody expects to love most.

  7. +2:10

    7. Little Havana

    Inspired · Calle Ocho

    Cafecito and a pastelito at a ventanita, then dominoes at Máximo Gómez Park. Slow down here: this is the part of Miami you will still be talking about on the flight home.

  8. +2:35

    8. Brickell

    Inspired · the skyline shots

    Glass towers stacked to the sky in Brickell, then back over the MacArthur Causeway past the cruise ships at PortMiami and home to the neon on Ocean Drive.
