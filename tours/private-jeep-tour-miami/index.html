Itinerary

  1. +0:00

    1. Ocean Drive

    Inspired · the neon hotel strip

    Meet on the Art Deco strip the first trailer’s look is built on: pastel hotels, neon signage and the Clevelander corner.

  2. +0:25

    2. Lummus Park Beach

    Inspired · the beach scenes

    The candy-colored lifeguard towers and the sand from the beach scenes. Crew photo stop.

  3. +0:40

    3. Venetian Causeway

    Inspired · the causeway drive

    Island-hop across the bay on the old drawbridge causeway, skyline rising ahead.

  4. +0:55

    4. Downtown & Biscayne Blvd

    Inspired · the arena and the club

    The waterfront arena, the color-changing crown of the tallest tower and the 24-hour superclub from the second trailer.

  5. +1:10

    5. Wynwood

    Inspired · the arts district

    A 25-minute walk through the murals and warehouse streets of the arts district.

  6. +1:40

    6. Little Haiti

    Inspired · the Caribbean quarter

    Caribbean color, murals and music. Stop by the cultural complex and taste a Haitian patty.

  7. +2:10

    7. Little Havana

    Inspired · Calle Ocho

    Cafecito and a pastelito at a ventanita, then the domino tables of Máximo Gómez Park.

  8. +2:35

    8. Brickell

    Inspired · the skyline shots

    The glass canyon of the financial district, then back over the MacArthur Causeway past PortMiami to Ocean Drive.
