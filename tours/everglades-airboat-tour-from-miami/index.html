Questions about this tour

Is transport from Miami included?

Yes. The tour includes a round-trip shuttle from Miami.

Will we see alligators?

Your wildlife guide takes you to the places gators are most often spotted, though wildlife sightings are never guaranteed.

Is it suitable for kids?

Yes, for ages 5 and up.

No. Leonida Tours is an independent South Florida tour company running unofficial tours. We take you IRL to the real places that inspired the game's world. We are not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
