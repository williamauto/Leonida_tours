Meeting Point: Where Every Tour Starts and How to Find Us

One address for the city tours, one gate for the boats, and what to do if you are fifteen minutes late. Written down, so you do not spend launch week wandering.

The most common reason a good trip goes badly is not the tour. It is the twenty minutes before it, spent on a street corner in Miami Beach looking for a minibus. Here is everything, in one place.

The city tour meeting point

1200 Ocean Drive, Miami Beach, FL 33139. That is the Moorington Hotel, the pale pink Art Deco building on the north side of the street between 12th and 13th Street, opposite the CVS. It has a coffee shop on the ground floor and the street parking outside it is metered.

  • The vehicles are white air-conditioned minibuses with Leonida Tours on the side in pink and teal.
  • Your guide is in a pink lanyard and will be standing at the kerb.
  • Come to the corner of Ocean Drive and 12th Street if the hotel entrance is hard to find.
  • The bus is usually parked a few doors down from the hotel on Ocean Drive itself, facing north.

The boat tour meeting point

Boats leave from the marina, not from Ocean Drive. The gate is at the end of the parking area on the island side of the bridge, and the address to put in your maps is the Miami Beach Marina on the causeway. Follow the road across the bridge until it ends, and turn into the marina on your left.

  • Look for the marina office building and the fuel dock. The boat is tied up beyond it.
  • Boarding is from a floating dock with a step ladder, so wear closed shoes.
  • Arrive 20 minutes early. The gate closes five minutes before departure for the manifest.
  • The walk from an Ocean Drive hotel to the marina gate is about 12 minutes on foot along the boardwalk, or a five-minute drive.

The Everglades and Keys meeting points

Both day trips use a shuttle, and the pickup is from a set of hotel stops rather than a single address, because it makes the route work for everyone.

  • Hotel pickup is from the lobby or the kerb outside the main entrance, whichever the driver prefers.
  • The stop list is in your confirmation email with times, and the bus usually has to leave the last stop by 8:00am.
  • If you are staying outside the pickup area, meet at the marina gate office and you will be collected there.
  • Do not be late. The Everglades drive is timed against the boat operators, and a late bus means a shorter ride.

If you are running late

Call the number on your confirmation as soon as you know you have a problem. The practical rules are these.

How lateWhat happens
Up to 20 minutesWe hold the departure. No penalty.
20 to 45 minutesWe will try to collect you, but the tour runs short by the time we pick you up.
More than 45 minutesTreat it as a cancellation and call ahead. We will move you to another date at no charge.

Traffic is the usual reason. If you are coming from the airport, from Brickell during rush hour, or across the causeway at 5pm on a Friday, allow 25 minutes more than the map says. The map does not know about the drawbridges.

What to bring

  • Sunscreen. Required on every outdoor tour and not optional in Miami in any month.
  • Water. We have it on board, but a personal bottle is better.
  • Closed shoes. Especially for the boats and the Everglades boardwalk.
  • A hat or a cap. The city tour has shade on the bus and almost none on the walk.
  • Light rain protection from June to September. It is usually a fifteen-minute downpour and then it stops.

Getting there

  • By car: Ocean Drive parking is metered and fills by 10am. There is a public garage on 13th Street and one on 12th, both a two-minute walk from the meeting point.
  • By rideshare: set the drop to Ocean Drive and 12th Street. Do not set it to the hotel name; the drop-off on Ocean Drive can be a long way round.
  • On foot: if you are on the strip, everything on this page is walkable.
  • By bus: the Miami Beach Trolley runs the strip all evening and stops on Ocean Drive, but it does not run early enough for the morning departures.

Frequently asked questions

Where do Leonida Tours meet for the city tour?

1200 Ocean Drive, Miami Beach, at the Moorington Hotel on the north side between 12th and 13th Street, opposite the CVS. Your guide is in a pink lanyard at the kerb.

Where do the boat tours leave from?

From the Miami Beach Marina, at the end of the road across the causeway. Turn left into the marina past the fuel dock. Boarding is from a floating dock with a step ladder.

How late can I be?

Up to 20 minutes late we hold the departure with no penalty. Between 20 and 45 minutes we will try to collect you and the tour runs short. Beyond that, call and we will move you to another date at no charge.

Is there parking at the meeting point?

Ocean Drive parking is metered and full by 10am. There are public garages on 12th and 13th Street, both a two-minute walk from the meeting point.

What should I bring on a tour?

Sunscreen, water, closed shoes, a hat, and light rain protection in summer. We have water on board but a personal bottle is better.

Do the Everglades and Keys tours pick me up from my hotel?

Yes, from a set of hotel stops listed in your confirmation email, and from the marina gate office if you are outside that area. The bus needs to leave the last stop by 8:00am.
